# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

GrowAgentAI is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping newcomers feel welcome

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Report to:** conduct@growagentai.com (placeholder)
2. **Include:** Who, what, when, where, witnesses
3. **Confidentiality:** Reports are confidential
4. **Response:** Within 48 hours

### Consequences

Violations will result in:
- **Warning** - First minor violations
- **Temporary Ban** - Harassment or repeated violations
- **Permanent Ban** - Severe or repeated violations

## Community Guidelines

### Be Respectful
- Treat all community members with respect
- Disagree respectfully
- Listen to understand, not to reply

### Be Inclusive
- Welcome new members
- Use inclusive language
- Consider diverse perspectives

### Be Constructive
- Provide helpful feedback
- Point out issues with solutions
- Celebrate contributions

### Be Honest
- Give credit to others' work
- Admit mistakes
- Communicate transparently

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Discord community
- GitHub Discussions
- Email communications
- Project events
- Social media (when representing project)

## Consequences for Violations

1. **Verbal Warning** - First incident, minor violation
2. **Temporary Mute** - 24-72 hours in Discord/GitHub
3. **Temporary Ban** - 1-4 weeks from community
4. **Permanent Ban** - Repeated violations or severe incidents

## Examples

### ✅ Good Behavior
```
"I disagree with your approach. Have you considered trying X? I think it might be more efficient."

"I'm new to this project. Can someone help me get started?"

"Great PR! I have a suggestion for improvement..."

"That's an interesting perspective I hadn't considered."
```

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior
```
"Your idea is stupid and you're dumb."

"You don't belong here."

"Stop wasting everyone's time with your questions."

[Sexualized comments or images]

[Harassment or threats]
```

## Addressing Grievances

If you feel you were treated unfairly:

1. **Appeal** to conduct@growagentai.com
2. **Explain:** Why you believe the decision was unjust
3. **Review:** Community leadership will reconsider
4. **Resolution:** Within 1 week

## Community Leaders

Community leadership is responsible for:
- Clarifying expectations
- Enforcing standards
- Taking action on violations
- Maintaining confidentiality
- Recusal from conflicts

## Commitment

We commit to:
- Being fair and impartial
- Protecting confidentiality
- Providing due process
- Learning and improving
