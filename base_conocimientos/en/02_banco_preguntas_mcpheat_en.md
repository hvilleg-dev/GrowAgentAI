# 02 — Bank of Coaching Questions (core, McPheat-based)

> Main source: "400 Coaching Questions" (Sean McPheat). Questions deduplicated and grouped by moment in the conversation. Where it says "the work / objective / goal" the agent must adapt to the person's life area (personal, studies, work, spiritual, family, health, finances).
> Usage rule for the agent: ask ONE question at a time, wait for the response, and choose according to the stage of the conversation (see file 01).

---

## A. CREATE GOALS (opening and session objective)
**When to use:** start of session or when the person doesn't know where to begin.

- How is everything going? (If they respond "fine/ok": "Tell me more about that 'ok'")
- What's on your mind?
- What do you want to change?
- What do you really want?
- If you had the perfect week/month, what would it look like?
- What would you like us to work on today?
- What would you like to achieve?
- What outcome are you trying to achieve?
- What is the biggest change you'd like to make right now?
- What would you like to happen by the end of this session?
- What would be the ideal outcome?
- What would be the most useful area to work on?
- What would be most helpful to take with you today?
- What could we work on that would help you most in the coming weeks?
- How will we measure the success of this coaching process?
- So, what is our goal for today?

**Follow-up between sessions**
- What progress have you made since our last session?
- What are the 3 biggest achievements since last time?
- What hasn't worked for you since our last session?
- What could have gone better in the time between sessions?
- What have you done since last time to move closer to your goals?
- What help do you need from me most today?

**Coaching agreement (first session)**
- Have you worked with a coach before? How did it go?
- What do you expect from this process together?
- What kind of coach do you need me to be to bring out the best in you?
- What kind of coach do you not want me to be?
- What do you need from me most during our sessions?
- What is the part that scares you most about working with me?

---

## B. EVALUATION PERIODS (year-end, cycle, or stage closure)
- What is your overall goal for the next year?
- What do you want to have achieved in 12 months?
- What is your biggest challenge for the next year?
- What are your most important priorities?
- What would make this year extraordinary for you?
- What would make this a good year? And what would make it a bad year?
- What do you need to do more / less / start doing / stop doing?
- If you had to give the next year a title, what would it be?

---

## C. EXPLORE THE "WHY" (motivation and meaning)
- Why do you want to achieve this goal?
- Why is this important to you? / Why does it matter?
- Why did you choose this particular goal?
- What will achieving it give you? / What will it allow you to do?
- What is the benefit of achieving it? What is the "return" on that investment?
- What difference would it make if you achieved it?
- Imagine you achieve it: describe what it would look like and what impacts it would have.
- Is this a goal of "should" or "must"?
- On a scale of 0 to 10, how serious are you about this goal?
- How exciting is this goal for you? Does it excite you when you think about it?
- What is your intuition about this goal?
- How will you feel if you don't achieve it? What impact would not achieving it have?
- What price are you willing to pay to achieve it?
- Where does it fit with your current priorities?
- What other areas of your life will be affected?
- How do others feel about this goal? How will it affect other people?
- Who else will benefit? Who will celebrate with you when you achieve it?
- Could your situation worsen if you achieve this goal?

---

## D. SPECIFIC GOALS (clarity, measurement, SMART)
- What is your goal in a few words? Describe it in one sentence.
- How can you make it clearer and more specific?
- How will you know you achieved it? How will you measure your success?
- Is it measurable? Is it achievable? Is it realistic?
- Is it completely within your control?
- What is the deadline?
- Can you divide it into smaller sub-goals?
- What milestones can you set to measure progress along the way?
- How can you evaluate it at the end?
- What would be a more ambitious version of this goal?
- If we're talking about a problem: can you give me your problem in one sentence?

---

## E. NEEDS AND CURRENT REALITY (where you are today)
- Where are you now? What is happening now?
- Where are you now in relation to your goal, and where do you need to be?
- What steps have you already taken? What have you tried? What progress have you made?
- What is working well? What isn't working for you?
- What problems or challenges are you facing right now?
- What is in the way of your success? What keeps you from achieving it?
- What are you tolerating / putting up with right now?
- What do you need to improve right now? What is missing from your life (work, personal)?
- What do you need to do more? What do you need to do less?
- What are you currently doing that isn't serving you?
- What excites, inspires, or energizes you today?
- If you could wave a magic wand, what would you wish for?
- What needs to change right now?
- What could we work on that would make the biggest difference?

---

## F. CREATE MOMENTUM FOR CHANGE (urgency and commitment)
- How important is this goal to you? What does it mean to you?
- What is there for you if you achieve it?
- What will happen if you stay the same and do nothing? How much will it cost you not to make this change?
- What will happen if you continue with your current course of action?
- Is there any benefit in doing nothing?
- What is the pain for you if you don't achieve this goal?
- What is an easy first step you could take?
- Who will you need to become to achieve it?
- What are you willing to sacrifice to make this change?
- How committed are you to making this happen?
- Have you ever abandoned a goal? How will you avoid repeating it?
- Is this goal exciting and challenging enough for you?
- How will you feel when you achieve it? And if you don't?
- How will you celebrate or reward yourself when you get it?

**Deep honesty questions (use with trust and warm tone)**
- What don't you want me to ask?
- Is there something you've been hiding or avoiding?
- What aren't you ready to change yet?
- What question do you think I should ask you about making this change?
- What are you afraid of?
- What's the worst that could happen? The best? The most likely?
- What paths have you explored to make this change?
- Who would you ask for good advice about this?
- If you could take a "magic pill" to move forward, what would it allow you to do?
- What do you suggest I ask you to keep you moving forward?

---

## G. CREATE ACCOUNTABILITY (accountability)
- How will you hold yourself accountable for this? How would you like to be accountable?
- How will I know your progress / that you're moving forward?
- Who else do you need to tell about this goal? Who else can help you be accountable?
- What commitment are you going to make? How can you make it visible?
- What consequence will you impose on yourself if you don't move forward?
- With whom can you make a commitment (agreement)?
- How will you stay committed to this goal?

---

## H. OPTIONS, DECISIONS AND ACTION
**Generate options**
- What are your options? What options do you have to move forward?
- What would work best for you?
- Which option do you like most? Which least? Which is easiest? Which is hardest?
- Which option has the biggest reward? And which has the biggest reward but most risk?
- What is the upside and what is the downside of each option?
- What else could you do? What could you do differently?
- What have you tried before? What worked and what didn't?
- Have you achieved goals like this before? What did you do?
- Do you know anyone who has achieved this? What steps did they take?
- What advice would your best colleague / your role model / yourself give? And why?

**Decide**
- What decision do you need to make? What decisions have you been avoiding?
- What decision would you make if you knew you couldn't fail?
- If you were 100% sure, what would you do?
- What does your gut / intuition tell you about each option?
- Which option do you feel ready to act on?
- What action is calling to you?

**Act**
- What do you think you should do next? What is the first thing you could do?
- What small step can you take now?
- When are you going to start?
- What action can you take right after this conversation? In the next 24 hours? This week? This month?
- What 3 actions can you take to get started?
- In an ideal world, what steps would you take to achieve this as quickly as possible?
- Imagine it's 1 week / 1 month / 1 year from now: what would you like to have achieved?
- What is the hardest part of this for you?
- If you summed up the situation in one word or phrase, what would it be?

**For mental blocks ("I don't know")**
- If something were possible, what would you do?
- If you did know, what would it be?
- What don't you know?
- Take your time: what do you think? What do you feel about it? What do you see?

---

## I. MAKE IT HAPPEN (resources, confidence, support)
- Can you achieve this on your own? How will you do it?
- What resources will you need? How will you get them? What resources and skills do you already have?
- What support do you need? How can I help you?
- What skills will you need to develop?
- What kind of person will you need to become? How do you need to change for this to happen?
- How will you stay on track?
- Do you have enough confidence? If you had 50% more confidence, what impact would it have?
- Have you decided to act or are you just hoping it will happen?
- If you had an extra hour each day, what would you do?

---

## J. FOLLOW-UP ON INACTION (when they didn't do what was agreed)
**Tone:** curious, without judgment. Never "Why didn't you do it?" in a reproachful tone.
- Where did you stop?
- What kept you from taking the step?
- How did you hold yourself back?
- Did something else take priority over this action?
- What did you do instead of your action?
- What impact has not acting had?
- How will you prevent this from happening again?

---

## K. BARRIERS AND OBSTACLES
- What could get in the way? What obstacles are you likely to encounter?
- What will be the hardest part?
- How could you derail or sabotage yourself?
- Is there anything that stops or prevents you from achieving it?
- What might you be assuming here?
- Is this a story or is it the truth?
- Do you use this to grow or to punish yourself?
- Is there something you need to let go of or release to achieve it?
- If other priorities come up, what will you do?
- What rules do you have that get in the way?
- Who holds you back here? Who drains you?
- Who else is affected or hurt by this?
- What is your mindset in facing this?
- How will you overcome any barrier? How will you support yourself to make this change?
- Are you willing to do whatever it takes?
- How much confidence do you have in moving forward?

---

## L. LIMITING BELIEFS (the deepest block; requires trust)
**Detect**
- What stops you with this? What do you believe stops you?
- Do you have any limiting beliefs about this? Which ones?
- Where does this belief/doubt come from? Who gave it to you?
- Are you getting in your own way? How?
- What are you avoiding? How does avoiding it affect you (your life, your work)?
- What emotions do you prefer to avoid feeling and why?
- If you weren't afraid, what would you do?
- Is it your head or your heart speaking?
- Are you trying to please someone? Who?

**Question**
- Does this belief give you power or take it away?
- Is it an unrealistic expectation? Are you giving too much importance to this thought?
- What evidence do you have? How do you know with 100% certainty it's true? Always? Every time without fail?
- Is it really valid today if it was formed long ago?
- What do you think these thoughts protect you from?
- If you could give a name to these thoughts, what would it be?
- What does this belief cost you? How does it hold you back?
- What have you tried to overcome it?
- Are you being too hard on yourself? List the reasons.
- What would become possible if you changed this belief?

**Replace**
- What new thoughts or beliefs could replace the old ones?
- What beliefs would serve you better and why? What evidence supports the new ones?
- How much more effective would you be / how much better would your life be without this belief?
- How can you teach others to treat you?
- Who would you benefit from surrounding yourself with to overcome these beliefs? Who have you hung around that undermines your confidence?
- What is the best and worst thing someone could tell you?

---

## M. REVIEW AND ADJUST (follow-up sessions)
- What progress have you made to date? What has worked? What hasn't gone as planned?
- What did you achieve that surprised you? What has been your biggest achievement? What are you most proud of?
- What have you learned? What could you have done differently?
- On a scale of 0-10, how do you feel about your goal now?
- What do you need to start / stop doing / do more / do less?
- What actions do you need to adjust? Do you need to adjust any goals? Why?
- How are you feeling about your timelines?
- What are your 3 biggest priorities now?
- What will your next action be? What is the most important thing you need to do next?
- How will you ensure this doesn't cool off?

---

## N. THOUGHT STARTERS (provocations)
- If you could only change one thing right now, what would it be?
- If you could stop doing only one thing, what would it be? And start one thing?
- What question do you think I should ask you next?
- If you were coaching yourself, what would you do? What would you ask yourself?
- Think of a role model: how would they handle this situation?
- Is there any decision or action you've postponed or avoided?
- Is doing nothing an option here?
- (Provocation, use only with high trust) "I don't think you really want to change. Convince me otherwise."

---

## O. QUESTION OPENINGS (templates to soften or vary tone)
Play with me here… · Imagine that… · I'm curious… · Have you ever…? · So tell me… · Step back and… · I wondered… · Let's suppose… · Just for a moment… · What if…? · Think of a time when… · Looking back… · Thinking forward… · On a scale of… · Rate yourself on… · If you were… · If you could… · In a nutshell… · Thinking big… · What skills…? · What support…? · What role do you play…? · "I'm going to interrupt you here…" (for someone who wanders).

---

## P. SESSION CLOSURE
- How was this conversation? How do you feel after it?
- Can you summarize what we covered?
- What are you taking with you from today? What were the key learnings?
- What is the first action you're going to take?
- What will you do differently from now on?

---

## Q. QUESTIONS TO AVOID (with example and alternative)
| Type | Problem | Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blame-inducing | Seek a culprit, not learning | "Whose fault is it?" | "What can we learn from this?" |
| Directive ("disguised advice") | Coach says what to do | "Why don't you do it this way?" | "What options do you see?" |
| Leading / tricky | Carry the answer included | "Aren't you going to talk to that person?" "Do you have problems with your client?" | "How is your relationship with your client?" |
| Rhetorical / judgmental | Criticize inaction | "If you don't have a plan, how will you do it?" "Why do you keep doing the same thing?" | "What would help you move forward?" |

## R. HOW TO USE AND DELIVER QUESTIONS (verified against McPheat's text)
What the text **does say**:
- Coaching is a process to facilitate the transition from where the person is to where they want to be (situation, goal, or project).
- The coach acts as a **facilitator** who explores options with the person; **it is not their function to give the answers**.
- **HOW you ask is as important as WHAT you ask**; if the how fails, the question won't have the desired impact.
- Questions should be **congruent with the coach's own language**: adapt them to your vocabulary.
- Treat the material as a guide ("swipe file"): consult it, choose a few questions, **try them out**, keep the ones that get good results, and discard the ones that don't work.
- **Always explore the why** of the goal (motivations of pleasure and pain); a goal without a reason is mere desire.
- **Close with focus:** don't let the session disperse; summarize learnings, actions, and next steps.
What the text **doesn't say** (don't attribute to McPheat): guidance on tone of voice, pauses, silence, active listening, or handling responses like "I don't know". It only offers a few template phrases for mental blocks (section H). Those techniques, when used in the agent, are own judgment or from other sources.
