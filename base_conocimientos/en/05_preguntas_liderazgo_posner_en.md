# 05 — Leadership, Growth, and Decision Questions (Posner and Weems, "Questions are the Answer")

> Use: work/professional stage and leadership (also applicable to family, community, student leaders, and entrepreneurs). Questions deduplicated; those repeated across chapters appear once. Adapt "team/organization" to the person's context.

## CHAP. 2 — GROW AS A LEADER

### Seek feedback (use with someone you trust)
- What is one thing you see me do (or fail to do) that holds me back?
- How can I do it better?
- What is the general perception of me?
- What could I do differently that would have the greatest impact on my success?
- How do my actions affect others?
- What is something I do exceptionally well that can help me address a weakness I have?
- Which parts of my team, organization, family, or surroundings know me best? Which know me least? Which do I know best? Which do I know least?
- What leadership qualities are most important in our organization?
- Can you give me feedback on how I've handled team dynamics so far?
- Which leaders in the company could I learn from? What development programs exist?

### Legacy and purpose
- When you've gone from this organization and they hang your portrait on the wall, what do you hope the bronze plaque below your portrait will say?
- What is the difference I want to make?
- Why is it essential to move in that direction, also for those who come after me and will inherit what I leave?
- What if my current job were not a stepping stone but a destination?

### Evaluate an offer or new role
- Do I care about this job (product, service, customer)?
- Is this opportunity congruent with my mission, values, and goals?
- Will I find meaning in performing the tasks of this position?
- Do I want to work with the people associated with this role?
- What new competencies will I need? How will I get them?
- What previous experiences can I draw from that are most similar to the new challenge?
- Who can I ask to be my mentor?

### Self-assessment
- If all workers in my profession did what I do, what would the world be like?
- What would lead someone to think that about you?
- If I were my supervisor, how would I describe myself?
- If someone asked the people I work with to describe me, what would they say?
- **Deming's 3 questions:** What is my job? What really counts in it? How well am I doing it?

### Reflection after an experience
- Did I handle this situation well?
- What did I do well? What could I have done differently?
- What have I learned from this experience? What can I learn to help me in the future?
- Is there a universal lesson here that others could apply?
- To accelerate, what am I willing to slow down?
- How could I avoid being in this situation again?

### Keep growing
- What is something I need to learn more about? Who can suggest sources? Where can I start that learning?
- What is a current behavior that, if I could change it, would make a significant positive difference in the quality of my leadership?
- **Weekly self-check:** How many barriers do people have to cross to speak directly with me? How much of my week do I spend out of my office or organization? How many questions do I ask versus statements I make? How many times this week have I said "I don't know" when asked a question?

## CHAP. 3 — FACE CHALLENGES

### First steps: define the problem
- What is not working and why?
- What problem are we trying to solve? Why is it important to solve it?
- What would success look like? What would be a short-term result that is challenging but achievable?
- What is possible and who cares?
- What is the system where there is a problem? Who has interest in that system? Do they understand they are part of it?
- What can I do in the short term? And in the longer term?
- What parts of the problem are under my control and which are not?
- What are my options and the pros and cons of each?
- Do we have the budget, skills, and staff to execute the solution?
- Where are we winning? Where are we still stuck?
- Are there steps we should be taking now to prepare for this eventuality?

### Think outside the box
- What if the opposite of what we believe were true?
- Do we do this because it works or because it's the way it's always been done?
- What assumptions are we making and what if we challenge them?
- What ideas would we try if we weren't afraid of failing?
- If budget, time, or resources were not a problem, how would we solve it?
- How would someone from a completely different industry approach it?
- What would we do if we had to deliver the solution in 24 hours?
- What is a bold move we've never considered but maybe should?
- How to achieve a 10x improvement instead of just 10%?
- What rule would we break (or rewrite) to do it better?
- What is new? What is next? What is better?
- Who else could we involve to bring new perspectives?
- What is happening in other industries or cultures that we could learn from?
- Are we limiting ourselves by thinking too narrowly? Are we solving the right problem or just the most obvious one?
- If we reframed this as an opportunity instead of a problem, what would change?
- What if we saw it from the customer's (or the other person's) perspective?
- What would a child or someone without experience in our field suggest?

### Identify resources
- Who else has had or has a similar problem and what can I learn from them?
- What is the big picture? How can I educate others about the broader context?
- How to give people time and space to discuss their answers?
- How to frame what is happening in a way that gives hope?
- What are the gaps in our knowledge on this topic?
- **For a very large problem, ask the group:** "This is truly a big problem. What do you think we need to know and do to solve it?"

### Connect with purpose
- WHY did we start doing WHAT we are doing in the first place?
- What are you trying to do? Who do you serve? (Greenleaf's two questions)
- Do we have a clear purpose (we will do X so that Y happens)? How did it help advance our mission? Did we reach our goal? What did we learn insofar as we didn't?
- Would there be a more effective way to achieve the goal?
- Do we regularly achieve the intended purpose? Do the results justify the time, energy, and resources invested?

### Monitor progress
- What has been completed? What is scheduled? What is late? Who opposes it? What can we do?
- Where are we now? Where do we need to be? What do we need to do to reach this goal?

### Evaluate performance
- What is the purpose of what we evaluate? What questions are we trying to answer? What information will we need and how will we get it? Is the plan logical, realistic, and practical?
- **Wheatley:** What happened? Why do we believe it happened? What can we learn from it?
- **Drucker:** If we hadn't done this already, knowing what we know now, would we start it?
- What does this say about our assumptions and approach? What will we do differently next time?
- What did we do well? What did we do poorly? What else should we have done?

### Foster learning
- What programs (habits, projects) thrive without extraordinary stimulus? Which struggle no matter what we do? What can we learn from them?
- To open a topic without criticizing: What do you know about…? Could you explain more about…? What has changed? Why? What are we going to do about it?
- How can we make this work even better?

### Creative abandonment (let go of what no longer serves)
- What things could we pause for a moment to observe what happens?
- What could be dismantled, reduced, or canceled without serious consequences?
- What rule, form, or procedure no longer serves a purpose? What processes no longer serve us?

## CHAP. 4 — LEAD CHANGE (adaptable to personal, family, or team changes)

### Identity (organization, team, family, or project)
- Why do we exist, beyond just making money?
- What is the one thing we constantly strive to achieve and that defines our ultimate contribution?
- What are our stated values? Which are actually lived in daily decisions and behaviors?
- What are the stories or pivotal moments that shaped what we are today?
- What do we do better than almost anyone else?
- How do people typically interact here with each other?
- What aspects of our current identity do we absolutely want to preserve as we grow or change?
- What makes me most proud to be part of this?
- When you hear [name], what word, phrase, or image comes to mind first? If someone asked you what people at [name] are like, what would you say?

### Plan change
- What emerging trend or need should we address next year? What won't change in the next 5 to 10 years?
- **Filter for a new initiative:** Is it valuable (worth the time)? Is it minimal (agile)? Is it understandable (clear)? Is it repeatable (model for other things)? Is it accessible (easily available)?
- What current strengths can we use for opportunities and challenges? What new skills do we need to develop?
- What one thing must happen for this effort to succeed? What one thing must NOT happen?
- What have you tried? What or who is in the way? What support do you need? What would you do if you were in my place? Is there anything else I should know?

### What if…? (vision and resistance to change)
- Imagine we're five years in and we've had incredible success. What specific results make us most proud?
- How does our ideal experience look in the future? If we appeared in a leading publication in five years, what would the headline say about our greatest achievement?
- What assumptions about [customers/market/environment] might no longer be true? What rooted beliefs ("this is how things work here") limit us?
- If we stopped doing [activity] completely, what would happen? What are we doing today that, if we started from scratch, we would never do?
- If a new competitor entered with unlimited resources, how would it alter the way we operate?
- What if we had no budget? What if it were unlimited?
- What if our most demanding customer were on the design team? What if we were a startup trying to disrupt our own industry?
- What if this solution had to be used by a 5-year-old or an 80-year-old? How would we simplify it?
- What if we eliminated the most obvious step or feature? What if we made the process "deliciously inefficient" to discover what is essential?
- What if a key resource disappeared or needs changed overnight? How would we adapt?
- What if we continue with the status quo? What persistent frustrations would remain unresolved?
- What if this change works better than expected? What positive ripple effects would it have on my work and personal development?
- What if there are unexpected challenges in the transition? How do we support each other? What if someone struggles to adapt? What support or training would be most helpful?
- What if we leveraged the unique experience of each person? What specific contribution could you imagine making?
- What if this change leads to more meaningful work, less frustration, or greater recognition for you? How would that feel?
- **Outcome scenarios:** What would be the ideal outcome? A good one? A terrible one? What if it doesn't work?

### Focus
- What is the single most critical individual outcome we absolutely must achieve? Why are we undertaking this change now?
- What is the central problem or key opportunity? How does "success" look specifically and how will we measure it?
- Does this activity directly contribute to our central result? If not, why are we doing it?
- What is the highest-leverage activity I could work on right now?
- What new information or challenges have emerged that require reevaluating our focus?
- What is the one thing we could stop doing that would free up energy and focus?
- What is currently diverting my attention from my most important work? What obstacles prevent me from focusing?
- Is there any task I can delegate or eliminate? Where do I spend time and energy not aligned with my priorities?
- What is the most critical thing you need to accomplish today or this week? How does it contribute to your goals?
- What are the most significant external distractions (notifications, noise, interruptions)? How do you manage them? What support do you need to reduce them?

### Learning agenda
- What else do I need to know and where can I look for it? What is worth verifying?
- How can I enrich and broaden the information I've received?
- What happens with the answer when I break it down or doubt the conclusions?

## CHAP. 5 — INNOVATION AND CREATIVITY

### Lead innovation
- How do I set the stage for innovation to occur?
- Will the way I handle this innovation increase, maintain, or decrease trust?
- What do we defend and why do we exist?
- Has the issue been set in people's minds? (test of "maturity" of the challenge)
- **What-When-Why message:** What do we want to achieve? When do we want it to happen? Why does it matter?
- What innovations do I see in similar organizations that deserve consideration?
- If someone new took my position today, what would they do that we're not doing now?
- If I were starting in this job, what would I do? What projects have I always wanted to undertake but never have?

### Foster creativity
- **Reversal:** If we wanted our sales (results) to decline next year, what things could we do? (then invert the answers)
- Question with: Why? Why not? What if…? (instead of just "how?")
- **Change perspective:** How would an admired organization (Disney, FedEx, the FBI…) handle this? How would our competitors respond? What are the questions I should be asking?
- If we had all the money in the world, how would we solve it? What if we had all the time? Assuming we don't have them, how can we scale down these solutions to work within our limitations?
- That's a good solution. What could be another?
- How fast do bad news travel upward? (test of openness)

### Evaluate the past and present
- Why do we do the things we do now? Why were current policies, practices, and processes implemented?
- In a world full of uncertainty, what are you sure of?
- What problems are you about to have and how can you solve them before they happen? What actions should you take now to have a better future?
- What are our strengths? Our capabilities? What risks do we dare take?

### Anticipate the future
- What is happening today that will change the world in the next 5 to 10 years?
- What question, if answered, could make the greatest difference for the future?
- What issues keep appearing on my radar?
- What things keep me awake at night or make me jump out of bed in the morning?
- What "I wish…" phrases do I find myself repeating?

## CHAP. 6 — MAKE GOOD DECISIONS

### First steps
- What is the main objective of the decision? What information do we need and how will we get it?
- Even if we achieve the results, will the time, effort, and money invested justify it?
- Why do we have this problem in the first place? What will happen if we decide not to decide?
- How can you expand your options? How to get out of your head and gather reliable information?
- How to overcome short-term emotions and conflicting feelings to choose well?
- How to plan for an uncertain future so your decision has the best chance?
- Before "what should we do?", ask: What could we do?
- Who disagrees with me and why? What are the best counter-arguments? What am I not considering, ignoring, or missing?

### Test assumptions
- What assumptions underpin my thinking and are valid? If they're wrong, how would the options change?
- Am I addressing the right problem or just the most obvious one? Am I choosing this option because it's safe and familiar, or because it's best?
- What assumptions are embedded in the plans? Which must prove true for it to succeed? How likely are they to be correct? Do we need to test them before proceeding?
- **Bregman's sequence for presenting an idea:** "Why won't this work for you?" → "Good point." → "So, how can you change it to make it work?"
- Why do we do it this way? What would happen if we stopped doing it completely?
- Who else has solved a similar problem and what can we learn?
- If our product/service/habit didn't exist, what would people do instead?
- What would a competitor do if they wanted to put us out of business? What could we create today that would have been considered impossible five years ago?

### Explore options
- Let's suppose we did it that way… what would happen?
- Is there a better way? What else could we do?
- **Badaracco's 5 questions:** What are the net consequences of all my options? What are my fundamental obligations? What will work in the world as it is? Who are we? What can I live with?
- **Disappearing Options Test:** If we can't choose any of the current options, what else could we do?
- **If there is fear:** What could happen if you take this course of action? What are the worst things? Why do those results scare you so much? How bad would it really be if they happen?
- What is a safe way to try an idea I've had long ago but never acted on?
- Have we overlooked any information or perspective?
- With persistent reservations: What are your greatest misgivings about this action? What other options do you see to achieve the same goal?
- **The question that breaks blocks:** What would I tell my best friend to do in this situation?
- If you risk accumulated goodwill: How much organizational capital (trust) do I have? How much am I risking? What are the probable rewards for others and for me?

### Evaluate impact
- **10/10/10:** How will we feel about this in 10 minutes? In 10 months? In 10 years?
- What is the upside if everything goes well? What is the downside if things go very wrong? Can I live with the downside?
- (Ethics) Not "how much can we get away with?" but rather: What are the consequences?
- Do I understand the questions, interests, and concerns of critical stakeholders? How should I adapt my message to each group?
- Afterward: Did the decision make a difference? What is worth repeating and what would I do differently?

## CHAP. 7 — PEOPLE, RELATIONSHIPS, AND DIFFERENCES
*(Recruitment questions are omitted as they are recruitment, not coaching. Interview questions are kept as self-discovery questions.)*

### Self-discovery questions (interview-type)
- Tell me about a mistake you made and how you handled it.
- Tell me about a setback or failure. What did you learn?
- Tell me about a moment when you achieved a successful result. What did you learn?
- What would you do even if you knew you could fail miserably?
- What do you like so much to do that the words failure and success become essentially irrelevant?
- Can you describe an occasion when you had to learn something new quickly?
- Describe a situation where you had to work with someone very different from you. What was challenging and what did you learn?
- If you were asked to improve [common situation], how would you approach it?
- What are the 3 to 5 key competencies this position (or life role) requires?

### Looking at yourself when choosing people
- Do I seek people with opinions different from mine? Do I associate with and learn from people different from me?
- Am I able to extract ideas that go beyond my interests and experience?
- Do I favor someone because they remind me of me or I like them? Would I feel different if the answer came from another group? Have I challenged my assumptions about what success looks like?

### Remove barriers and support others
- What barriers can I remove? In what ways can I involve people? How can I better appreciate and develop people?
- What do we do that gets in the way of you doing your job well?
- What rule, process, or policy, if removed, would make us more effective?
- How can I help? Is there anything you need now to perform better?
- What do I do that makes it difficult or blocks you? What could I stop doing? What should I keep doing?
- If it were in my power, what would you like me to do that I'm not doing?
- Where do you need me most and where least?
- What is blocking your progress? What outcome do you aspire to? What would you like me to do to help?
- After helping: Was my assistance helpful? What else could I have done?

### Engage and retain (commitment conversation)
- What is going well? What recent success has meant a lot to you?
- What is your most important goal in the coming months? What do you need to achieve it?
- What would make you want to stay longer here? Can you imagine something happening that would make you consider leaving? What external factors could attract you elsewhere?
- What is the one thing you work on that will require you to grow to achieve it?
- Are there lessons we can learn from your previous workplace?
- How do we compare to what we said we would be?

### Handle differences and conflicts
- What information do your comments base on? What experience convinced you of this?
- What leads you to say that? Can you tell me more about what leads you to that conclusion?
- Can you give me an example or specific detail that helps me understand your point?
- What aspects of my perspective resonate with you, even if you don't fully agree?
- What do you think is the most significant difference between our opinions?
- If we found a solution that considered both points of view, what would it look like?
- What are your non-negotiables and what areas are open to compromise?
- Is there any common ground, however small, that we both agree on?
- What assumptions might we be making that influence our opinions?
- What are the potential benefits of considering an alternative approach?
- What would have to happen for everyone to feel comfortable with [solution]?
- How can we ensure different concerns are addressed in the decision?
- Can you help me understand the points most important to you here?
- Why do you think we haven't done something like this before? What would have to change if we implemented it? Who have you consulted already?
- That's an interesting idea. Can you give an example? What experiences shaped your perspective?

## CHAP. 8 — PLAN, PRIORITIZE, DELEGATE, AND MANAGE TIME

### Planning and purpose
- What do you think we should start doing? Stop doing? Keep doing? What do you think?
- Why does this keep happening?
- What are my fundamental beliefs and assumptions about life and work that keep recurring? What themes repeat?
- What social or charitable causes do I support?
- Why do we do what we do? Why does it matter?
- What ideals do I like about this organization (or project, or community)?
- What higher-order values give meaning and purpose to your life and work?
- **Bottom question:** Are you in this job to do something, or to have something to do? If it's to do something, what is it?

### Meetings and conversations worth having
- Do we need to meet? What outcome do we want from this meeting?
- Given our responsibilities, what is the most important thing we should discuss?
- What is something you have on your mind and haven't had the chance to say?
- At closing: How do you feel about what we accomplished? What is the big issue or learning you're taking with you?
- What are the next steps? Who will be responsible? By when? How will we hold each other accountable?
- Facilitator self-check: Were there distractions or side conversations? Who talked more, me or one or two people? Did the discussion stray? Were all opinions very similar (lack of idea diversity)?

### Delegate and let go of control
- Who has the skills to do this task well? For whom would this be a welcome challenge?
- How could I involve someone else to accomplish the task and develop them?
- Follow-up: Can you walk me through the steps you've taken? How far along is it compared to what you thought? What steps have been easier or harder than expected?
- **For those who struggle to delegate:**
  - What is the worst scenario if this isn't done exactly my way and how likely is it?
  - In what way does doing this myself prevent me from focusing on the most strategic?
  - What new challenges could I take on if I delegated successfully?
  - What do I gain by holding on to this task and what do I lose?
  - What part really requires my unique expertise?
  - If someone can do it 80% as well as me, is that "good enough"?
  - What is a small, low-risk task I can delegate first to build trust?
  - How can I set the person up for success instead of focusing on possible failure?
  - If I don't trust my team, what steps can I take to build that trust?
- **If something went wrong:** Did I delegate to the wrong person? Was it too much for one person? Were my expectations unrealistic? Did I give enough guidance? Did I monitor and stay available?

### Time management
- Do I have my three priorities clear for today or this week? If not, what is unclear?
- Did I spend time planning my day? How effective was it?
- Do I distinguish between urgent and important? Do I devote enough time to what is important but not urgent?
- What tasks do I constantly postpone and why (lack of clarity, skill, interest, or other)?
- What can I say "no" to without compromising my goals?
- What did I do this week or month that, knowing what I know now, I wouldn't take on again?
- Before accepting something: Am I the right person? Is it the right time? Do I have enough information? What would happen if I didn't do it? What would be the worst?

## CHAP. 9 — COMMUNICATION AND LISTENING

### Self-reflection of the communicator
- Do I listen more than I speak? Do I listen to understand or just wait for my turn?
- Am I listening to what they're really saying? Do I invite diverse opinions? Do I use open questions?
- Am I accessible? Do people feel comfortable coming to me with questions or concerns?
- Am I clear and concise? Do I use jargon or complex language when something simple would suffice?
- Do I adapt my message to my audience (their background, knowledge, and needs)?
- Do I ensure understanding or take it for granted? How do I verify it (open questions, ask them to paraphrase)?
- Are my expectations clear (what is expected, roles, outcomes)?
- Do I consider the emotional impact of my messages? Do I connect at a human level, beyond tasks?
- Do I recognize and validate others' perspectives even if I disagree?
- Do I use the right channel for the message (in person for sensitive, written for updates, messaging for quick)?
- Do I communicate consistently? Are there contradictory messages? Do I communicate too much or too little? How often do I share critical information and with whom?

### Make connections
- Beyond your immediate tasks, what excites you most to learn or develop? What challenge are you passionate about solving?
- How is [your daughter/family/health]? (sincere personal connection)
- It seems you're concerned about X and Y. Is that right? (verify what you heard)
- What do I want to achieve? If I were in their place, what would I want to know? What does my audience want to take with them?
- Who do we need to consult with for this change to work? Who needs to be informed? Who will be affected? Who should be involved? What is the best way to communicate with each?
- Does what I say align with their observations? Am I listening well? Did I miss something?
- What outcome would make this conversation a success for you?

### Deep listening and psychological safety
- Thank you for pointing that out; I see you feel pressure. When you say "[phrase]", can you elaborate on what specific challenge you encounter?
- On a scale 1-10, how comfortable are you taking a risk or making a mistake here? Why?
- Do you feel safe sharing your opinion, even if it goes against the majority or against mine?
- When we make a mistake, do we learn collectively or look for someone to blame?
- Do you feel comfortable asking for help when you're stuck?
- If you had a concern, how sure are you it would be heard and addressed constructively?
- What could we do to make it safer to share ideas and concerns?
- Can you explain it another way? What do you mean by [key term]? Can you give me an example of how it looks in practice?
- What do you think is the central problem? What are all the possible causes? In how many ways could we address it?
- If we tried [solution], what challenges or unintended consequences could there be?
- What perspectives are missing from this conversation? Who else should be at the table?
- How do we make people from different backgrounds feel safe sharing? What mechanisms do we have to gather input from everyone?

### Expand the circle (get out of the bubble)
- What is a new activity, role, or challenge that stretches my boundaries?
- Who (inside or outside) does something completely different and what can I learn?
- When was the last time I worked on something I wasn't yet good at?
- Who do I rarely talk to who could offer a fresh perspective?
- What networks or communities am I not part of but should be?

### Shared vision
- What values do you think are most essential to maintain? What is our greatest strength right now?
- What kind of impact should we want to have in the community?
- Imagine we have incredible success within [timeframe]. What does it look like? What have we achieved?
- What principles or beliefs should guide us? How do we ensure the vision reflects all our perspectives?

## CHAP. 10 — MAKE A DIFFERENCE (purpose, intentionality, and priorities)

### Purpose and values
- What kind of impact do I want to have? When do I feel most alive and effective?
- What values guide my decisions, even under pressure? What are my five most important values?
- If I left tomorrow, what legacy would I expect to leave?
- What do I believe when it comes to people and relationships?
- In hindsight, did I make the right trade-offs between competing values?
- Can I live with myself doing what the organization (or my surroundings) is asking?
- Have I made promises I realistically can't keep? What do I need to do if there is a disconnect?
- Do I feel comfortable being myself, even admitting when I don't have all the answers?
- How does my personal purpose connect to the broader purpose of this organization/community?
- Beyond immediate goals, what long-term impact do I want to have on people, the environment, and the community?
- How does that desired impact influence my daily priorities? How am I articulating this vision to inspire and align others?

### Intentionality
- Where do we want to go? How will we know we're getting there?
- What does the situation demand?
- Given my strengths, my way of performing, and my values, how can I make the most significant contribution to what needs to be done?
- What results must be achieved to make a difference?
- Are initiatives launched without success metrics? Who evaluates the initiative's success: only those who launched it?
- If everything else remained the same, what is the one area where change would have the greatest impact?
- How does your idea contribute to our goal?

### Priorities and own role
- What is one thing I can do to advance [the goal] today or this week?
- What is that one thing that, given my perspective and resources, only I can do?
- What can I say "no" to so I can say "yes" to something more important?
- Do I let others' priorities dictate my urgency, or do I set my own?
- **Quadrants Matrix (urgent/important):**
  - Which quadrant do I operate in most? (Q1 Urgent-Important · Q2 Not urgent-Important · Q3 Urgent-Not important · Q4 Not urgent-Not important)
  - Q1: How much time do I spend in crisis? Which are recurring and what proactive step would move them to Q2 (prevention)?
  - Q2: How much time do I invest in important but not urgent? What would happen if I doubled that time?
  - Q3: What do I do that is urgent but unimportant? Who or what drives those demands? How can I delegate, reject, or limit them?
  - Q4: How much time is wasted and what value does it add?
- Can I really fulfill this request with the time and resources I have? Is it the most important thing I should do now?
- **New role or position:** What is its purpose and why does it exist? What results are expected? What are the responsibilities? How does it fit in the broader picture? How will I establish my support network in my new location or role?
- What advice do you have for making our board/team more effective next year?

## AUDIT SUPPLEMENTS (verified against actual book index)

### Chap. 3 — "Testing Routines" (review routines and procedures)
*How to use:* periodically put the first question on the team agenda and invite each person to bring their best answers; then choose an option as a group. Every six months, ask yourself.
- What rule, form, or procedure no longer serves a purpose?
- Are there meetings that are not necessary?
- What presentations can be shortened?
- Can approvals be simplified?
- Can we eliminate some forms or documents?
